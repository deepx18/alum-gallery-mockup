About Northline

A small senior team, embedded in your product.

Northline is a studio of designers and engineers who prefer long engagements to one-off launches. We join the stand-up, share the roadmap and stay accountable for what we ship — whether that's a new platform or the messy middle of an existing one.

Our expertise

  • Discovery & research

    Interviews, analytics and competitor work that turn opinions into a brief.

  • Design systems

    Tokens, components and documentation your team can build on without us.

  • Web & app engineering

How we work

Four stages, no surprises

Short cycles, visible progress and a decision log you can hand to the next team.

  1. 01

    Discover

    Understand the business, the users and the constraints. Agree on what success looks like.

  2. 02

    Design

    Sketch, prototype and test the flows that matter most before committing to build.

  3. 03

    Build

    Ship in two-week cycles with reviews, staging previews and real content from day one.

  4. 04

    Improve

    Measure what launched, then iterate on the parts that are actually being used.
